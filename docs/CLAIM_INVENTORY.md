| D01 | 89–93 | Ambient space is the finite-dimensional real Euclidean space `R^d`; `d` is a nonnegative integer under the usual convention. | D03 | 95–103 | A norm `ν` is an actual norm on `R^d` (positive definite, absolutely homogeneous for real scalars, subadditive). `K_ν={x:ν(x)≤1}`. `x_ν(u)=u/ν(u)` for Euclidean unit vectors `u`. | D06 | 215–224 | Analytic Fourier and weighted spaces have complex scalars. `C_0(R^r)` means continuous complex functions vanishing at infinity. The ordinary `sup/inf` statements in the source can be rendered by their equivalent uniform epsilon formulations on these nonempty approximation classes: `∀ε>0, ∃n₀, ∀n≥n₀, ... ∃p, ∀x∈boundary, error<ε`. For a fixed epsilon, the threshold occurs **before** the body, norm, and centre in the uniform assertions. Replacing uniformity by a body-dependent threshold, replacing all sufficiently large degrees by a subsequence, replacing a strict uniform-sup bound by unrelated pointwise bounds, or restricting `L` to natural numbers changes the statement. Pointwise strict bounds imply a strict supremum bound on a compact sphere only after continuity and compactness are established; a direct epsilon-margin formulation avoids that inference.
